Our Story

Founded on a simple idea: keep it under one roof

United AtoZ Printing Service was built on a straightforward idea: stop sending customers elsewhere for the parts of a print job we don't handle ourselves. Most print shops across the Kathmandu Valley cover one piece, design, or print, or signage, and leave the rest to someone else. We built United AtoZ so a customer could walk in with just an idea and walk out with a finished product, a stack of business cards, a shop signboard, or a batch of branded t-shirts, all made in the same place. The shop has grown over the years into a formally registered, full-service printing business trusted by clients across Bhaktapur, Kathmandu and Lalitpur.

Today we work with businesses, schools, event organizers and individuals throughout the Kathmandu Valley. The shop runs 24 hours a day, because print deadlines rarely wait for regular business hours. A same-day order at midnight matters just as much as one placed at noon, in Bhaktapur, Kathmandu or Lalitpur.
